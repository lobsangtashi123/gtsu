Our Origins

The journey of GTSU began with a voluntary initiative called "Let's Open the Door to College" in 2016. This grassroots movement was started by Tibetan college students across India who recognized the challenges faced by +2 graduates in choosing suitable academic paths.

Through modern digital platforms, our team of students from India and the USA provided guidance to over 700 students within just two months, helping them make informed academic and career choices.

Our Mission

GTSU stands on three fundamental pillars:

  • Unity: Building strength through community
  • Education: Advancing knowledge and skills
  • Motivation: Inspiring positive change

Our Story

From Volunteers to Global Movement

GTSU began in 2016 as "Let Open The Door To The College" - a group of Tibetan college students who witnessed their juniors struggling to navigate college admissions. Through digital platforms, we connected +2 graduates with experienced mentors, helping over 700 students find their academic path. Today, we continue this mission of unity, education, and motivation.
