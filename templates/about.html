Our Story

Global Tibetan Student Union - Empowering Youth Through Unity

The history of human civilization has proved that unity is power. We, Tibetan students, have come together under the umbrella of Global Tibetan Student Union (GTSU) - built on three pillars of unity, education, and motivation among youths and humanity at large.

Our Beginning

Founded July 6, 2016

GTSU was initially known as "Let Open The Door To The College" - a voluntary initiative by college students helping +2 graduates from Tibetan schools across India navigate their academic journey.

We mark July 6th as our founding day, a birthday gift and deep gratitude to His Holiness the 14th Dalai Lama on his 81st birthday, honoring his tireless work for Tibet and world peace. This symbolizes our commitment as youth to take responsibility for our community and humanity.

GTSU is not here to compete, but to provide sincere voluntary services to students in need and cooperate with all who work for education and the betterment of humanity.
